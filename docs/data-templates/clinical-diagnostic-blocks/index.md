# Clinical Diagnostic Blocks


Clinical and Diagnostic Blocks capture patient health information generated during care delivery.

These blocks include:
- Clinical observations and assessments
- Diagnoses and medical history
- Laboratory and radiology investigations
- Medications and treatment plans

They are central to clinical decision-making and interoperability with EMRs, LIMS, and diagnostic systems.

## Structure of Clinical Diagnostic Blocks

```text

└── clinical-diagnostic-blocks
    ├── self-monitoring-clinical-information
    ├── clinical-information
    ├── allergies
    ├── chronic-conditions
    ├── lab-request-details
    ├── diagnosis-details
    ├── medication-details
    ├── treatment-details
    └── radiology-details
```
